# T0.6 — Generalization rule (industry vs client)

Status: draft · Phase 0 · English (service doc)

## Purpose

Decide what stays generic in the SME template and what a client customizes
(plan §2.8: "unified library at subprocess level + `decomposition` stubs;
client work = expanding operations"), and define the `decomposition` policy.

## Decision 1 — 4-criteria checklist ("industry" = stays generic)

An element stays in the template only if it passes **all four**:

1. **Universal** — occurs in essentially every SME of the industry regardless of
   plant, product mix or org chart.
2. **Token-free** — no client token: no brand, equipment model, real figure,
   target, plant code or person name (plan §3, const. 7).
3. **Structural** — describes the *flow / responsibility / step structure*, not
   the plant's specific data or nomenclature.
4. **Stable** — not tied to a temporary project, a specific org chart, or a
   one-off event.

Fails any criterion → it is a **client customization**, kept out of the template
(or reduced to a stub).

## Decision 2 — `decomposition` policy

`decomposition` (ontology §4.4) values and meaning in the template:

| Value | Template holds | Client work | Example |
|-------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| `ATOMIC` | the operation, complete & generic | none | `Проверка наличия сертификатов` |
| `DECOMPOSABLE` | a **stub** operation (goal + typed I/O, no inner steps) | expand into concrete sub-operations | `Выполнение технологических операций` |
| `PARTIALLY_DECOMPOSABLE` | some concrete children + a stub remainder | complete the remainder | `Проведение замеров` (generic) + equipment-specific steps |

**Default at subprocess-level v1 (plan §2.8/§5-T1.5):** every SUBPROCESS is a
stub (`decomposition: DECOMPOSABLE`) unless its operations are universal
(`ATOMIC`). `ATOMIC` operations are fully specified in the template.

## Decision 3 — ATOMIC vs DECOMPOSABLE examples

- `ATOMIC` (keep fully): QUAL-03-01-01 «Проверка наличия сертификатов»;
  PLAN-01-03-01 «Формирование сменного задания»; an approval step
  «Согласование и утверждение».
- `DECOMPOSABLE` (stub in template): MFG-01-02 «Выполнение технологических
  операций» (depends on the specific technology — externalized to
  `technologies/*.md`); MFG-02-02 «Выполнение переналадки» (equipment-specific).
- `PARTIALLY_DECOMPOSABLE`: QUAL-03-02-01 «Проведение замеров» — generic
  sampling steps fixed, instrument-specific steps expanded per client.

## Verification

- 4-criteria checklist defined; ATOMIC / DECOMPOSABLE examples given.
- Rule: at v1 freeze (T1.5), subprocess-level files carry only stubs +
  `ATOMIC` operations; nothing client-specific.
