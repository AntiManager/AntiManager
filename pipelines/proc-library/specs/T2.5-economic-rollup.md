# T2.5 — Economic roll-up (reference FTE and layer cost)

Status: draft · Phase 2 · English (service doc)

## Purpose

Turn the T2.4 reference economics into a reproducible roll-up (the plan's
"economic check"): FTE and layer cost for every subprocess, process and group
(domain) of the **Planning** pilot, on the metric spine of T0.5 Decision 2.
Every value is a **reference value** for an SME template — never a client
figure (T0.6 Decision 1).

## Decision 1 — formulas

For a node `v` over its descendant operations `i`:

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Operations count | `N(v) = Σ_i 1` |
| Labour, ч/год | `Hours(v) = Σ_i Трудоёмкость_i × F_i` |
| **FTE** | `FTE(v) = Hours(v) / AnnualFund` |
| **Layer cost**, руб/год | `Cost(v) = Σ_i Стоимость_i × F_i` |
| Criticality | `max(Критичность_i)` (`high > medium > low`) |
| Periodicity | highest-frequency cadence present in the subtree |

Reference constants (T2.4 Decision 3): `AnnualFund = 1700 ч` per FTE,
`REF_RATE = 1000 руб/ч`; `Стоимость_i = Трудоёмкость_i × REF_RATE`, so
`Cost(v) = Hours(v) × REF_RATE` and
`Cost(v) = FTE(v) × AnnualFund × REF_RATE` exactly.

## Decision 2 — executions per year `F_i`

`F_i` = (cadence unit factor) × count, with `Периодичность = <unit> ×<count>`:

| unit | executions / year |
|------|-------------------|
| shift | 3 × 365 = 1095 |
| day | 365 |
| week | 52 |
| month | 12 |
| quarter | 4 |
| year | 1 |
| event | `count` (explicit, on demand) |

## Decision 3 — matrix and scope

The roll-up is emitted as one row per node at three levels — **subprocess →
process → group (domain)** — restricted to nodes that carry economics. Columns:
`code, level, name, ops (econ/total), Hours ч/год, FTE, Cost руб/год,
criticality, periodicity`.

`FTE` in the matrix is **annual FTE** (person-count), not a per-execution
figure. The matrix is **additive**: a parent row equals the sum of its children,
because every level sums the same leaf operations.

Tool: `.kilo/process-engine/rollup.py <library> [--scope PREFIX …] [--json out]`.

## Worked roll-up (T2.5 verification target)

Planning pilot (`--scope PLAN SL_PLAN`), 66 operations:

| code | level | ops | Hours ч/год | FTE | Cost руб/год | crit/period |
|------|-------|-----|-------------|-----|--------------|-------------|
| PLAN | group | 49 | 15 740,2 | 9,259 | 15 740 250 | high/shift |
| PLAN-01 | process | 13 | 6 516,5 | 3,833 | 6 516 500 | high/shift |
| PLAN-02 | process | 16 | 9 033,8 | 5,314 | 9 033 750 | high/shift |
| PLAN-03 | process | 20 | 190,0 | 0,112 | 190 000 | high/quarter |
| SL_PLAN | group | 17 | 2 973,5 | 1,749 | 2 973 500 | high/shift |
| SL_PLAN-01 | process | 8 | 188,0 | 0,111 | 188 000 | high/week |
| SL_PLAN-02 | process | 7 | 2 737,5 | 1,610 | 2 737 500 | high/shift |
| SL_PLAN-03 | process | 2 | 48,0 | 0,028 | 48 000 | medium/month |
| **Σ pilot** | — | **66** | **18 713,8** | **11,008** | **18 713 750** | high/shift |

`Σ PLAN + Σ SL_PLAN = 15 740 250 + 2 973 500 = 18 713 750 руб/год`; the
subprocess level sums to the same total (additivity holds at all three levels).
The T2.4 control set (`PLAN-01-01`, 5 × `week ×1`) reproduces its sample
exactly: `FTE = 0,138`, `Cost = 234 000 руб/год`, criticality `high`.

## Verification

- `rollup.py --scope PLAN SL_PLAN` → the matrix above, finite and reproducible.
- Falsifiable invariants (`.kilo/process-engine/test_engine.py`):
  - additivity: `FTE(parent) = Σ FTE(children)` at every level;
  - identity: `Cost(v) = Hours(v) × REF_RATE` and
    `Cost(v) = FTE(v) × AnnualFund × REF_RATE`;
  - control set: `PLAN-01-01` = 0,138 FTE / 234 000 руб/год / `high`;
  - pilot total = 66 ops, `FTE 11,008`, `Cost 18 713 750 руб/год`.
- Determinism: two runs yield identical `--json` output.
