## Key findings (evidence-backed)
1. **No site search** anywhere (grep of `build.js`, `brutalist.js`, all pages). Corpus:
   23 weapon landings + 23 `/full/` texts + 14 articles + 5 doc pages + 4 genome pages.
   Full texts average ~60 KB / 11 H2. This is the single biggest navigation gap.
2. **Cross-library flow is one-way.** Articles link to `/weapons/` (14/14), but
   **23/23 weapon pages never link to `/articles/`**; `weapons.json` has no `related`
   field. Weapons also don't link to `/cases/` stories, `/genome/`, or `/full/` siblings
   beyond "В том же окопе" (same zone only).
3. **Arsenal discovers ~17 of 23/24 weapons.** `scenarios.json` covers 16 of 23 weapons
   (crisis 4, team 2, changes 4, system 6); 7 weapons have `zone: null` and appear in no
   scenario: **03, 07, 09, 12, 17, 21, 23**. Homepage "участок фронта" links all four
   zones to `/scenarios/` — a showcase page, not a route (same defect the 2026-07 review
   flagged).
4. **Star Map is decorative.** `#system-map` renders `starSvg()` with no links; the hero's
   secondary CTA points at it, so its payoff is an image, not navigation.
5. **Dead-end content.** `/cases/` = 10 cards, static, no per-story pages; `/archive/` =
   9 thinker cards, no links (`thinker-arrow` "→ PDCA" is plain text). Both terminals
   have zero onward routes.
6. **Minimal pages.** `/about/` repeats the tagline twice with no team/credentials/format;
   `/headquarters/` is one paragraph + one Telegram button.
7. **Homepage narrative before first route.** 492 visible words; hero → archaeologists →
   weapon grid → **then** Star Map/zones. Hero CTA "ХОЧУ ПОНЯТЬ СИСТЕМУ" scrolls to a
   decorative map instead of listing tools.
8. **Data bug.** `tools.json`: item `{ "id": "trizy", ..., "slug": "triz" }` — id/slug
   mismatch; also `teoriya-ogranicheniy` is mislabeled ("неограниченной рациональности").
9. **Disconnected zones.** Arsenal + scenarios use 4-zone vocabulary (crisis/team/changes/
   system); `/genome/` uses 5-contour vocabulary (стратегия/процессы/информация/люди/
   адаптация). Two mental models, no bridge. `/genome/` CTAs go to `/contact/?pack=…`
   while `/headquarters/` exists as a weaker duplicate CTA target.
